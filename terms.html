Community rules

Terms & Conditions

Ground rules for accounts, proposals, administrator decisions, and respectful play.

Last updated: 10 October 2026

1. Nature of Fortune Apex

Fortune Apex is a private community game and record-keeping website. Homemade currency and balances are fictional game units only. No real-money deposits, cash-outs, or payments are offered.

2. Accounts

Use a unique username and a password you do not reuse elsewhere. Registration requests may remain pending until approved. Keep login credentials and password-recovery links private.

3. Permissions and proposals

Members can use their own account features and submit proposals. They may not access private records or change official rules, rankings, or records without authorisation. Submitting an idea does not make it an official rule; changes require the applicable administrator review.

4. Administrator decisions

Administrator actions are governed by the site's role controls and review workflow. Administrator accounts should be separate. Do not share administrator passwords or bypass approval requirements.

5. Acceptable use

Do not bypass access controls, impersonate members, tamper with records, spam forms, exploit bugs, attempt to access another user's account, or interfere with service operations.

6. Security logs and IP addresses

For security and accountability, the application records selected security and administrative events. These records may include an IP address, account identifier, timestamp, action type, and related event details. Events may include successful or failed sign-ins, installation activity, and administrator or account-management actions.

IP addresses may also be used as inputs to rate-limiting checks. Authorised administrators with access to the audit trail may review events for security, moderation, troubleshooting, and accountability. The interface shows the latest 100 events; older entries may remain in the database.

The application does not automatically delete audit records on a fixed schedule. Hosting-provider logs may be handled separately. See the Privacy Policy for collection and retention information.

7. Availability

This is a hobby project and may be changed, paused, or unavailable. Do not rely on it as a financial ledger or source of real-money value.

8. Changes and contact

These terms may change as the community evolves. Contact the administration team through the Contact page.

These terms are a starter template, not legal advice. Review them against the actual service and applicable requirements before launch.
